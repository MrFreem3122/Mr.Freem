About the project

GadgetTrend 241 started as an e-commerce business: I source gadgets from suppliers, test which products people want, and sell them online. The website is where customers browse the catalogue and place their orders.

The front end and the back end both live in one Next.js app, with orders and products stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted on Neon. A test version is deployed on Vercel while I finish the remaining features.

What I built

  • Product catalogue with categories and search
  • Cart and checkout in the shop's own colours
  • Customer accounts
  • Orders saved in a PostgreSQL database
  • Deployment on Vercel with a Neon database

Coming next

  • An admin dashboard to manage orders, products, stock, customers and deliveries
  • New-order alerts by email and WhatsApp
  • Online payment with Mobile Money (Airtel Money, Moov) and bank card
